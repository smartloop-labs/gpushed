FAQ

Questions

Why Mac only?

gpushed's client runs inside Apple's native container runtime, which needs a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 26 or later. Focusing on one platform keeps setup to a single command.

What do I need to install?

Just gpushed. Its setup downloads Apple's container runtime from Apple's GitHub releases, checks Apple's signature and installs it, asking for your password once. No Terraform, gcloud, Python or GPU drivers on your Mac.

What's on the box?

Ubuntu 22.04 with the NVIDIA driver and CUDA Toolkit 12.6. Nothing else, so you can install PyTorch, JAX, vLLM or whatever your work needs, with full root access.

Where does it run?

On Google Cloud, in the region nearest you: Iowa, South Carolina or Oregon in the US, the Netherlands, or Singapore. If it's out of GPUs, gpushed tries its other zones and then the next nearest region automatically. Pick one yourself with gpushed up --region europe-west4. Prices differ a little by region; gpushed plans shows yours.

What happens when my credit runs out?

You get an email when your balance drops to $2. Just before it reaches zero your box is stopped, with its disk kept. Top up and run gpushed start to resume. A box that stays unfunded for 24 hours is deleted.

Can I keep my data between sessions?

Yes. gpushed stop pauses the GPU and keeps the disk for about $0.03/hour; gpushed start brings it back. gpushed down deletes the box and its disk for good.

How is it billed?

Per second, from your prepaid balance, from the moment the box exists until it's deleted. gpushed history shows every charge and top-up.

Can I run more than one box?

One box per account for now. Delete it with gpushed down to switch plans.
